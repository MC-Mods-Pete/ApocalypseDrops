ApocalypseDrops Changelog
=========================

1.0.2-Forge-mc1.20.1
--------------------
New
 - First release for Forge (Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47 or newer).
 - Zombies and Husks now also drop items that are useful in a zombie apocalypse. Every item has its own drop chance in the config.
 - Settings are in config/apocalypsedrops-common.toml: drop chance per item and switches for Zombie and Husk drops.
 - Needs no additional mods.
